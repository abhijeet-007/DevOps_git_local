Explore Manali

Where Mountains Meet Peace ❄️🏔️

Why Visit Manali?

🌿 Nature

Snowy peaks, green valleys, rivers, and breathtaking views.

🪂 Adventure

Paragliding, skiing, trekking, river rafting, snow activities.

🧘 Peace

A perfect escape from city life into calm mountain vibes.

Best Time to Visit

☀️ Summer (Mar – Jun)

Best weather for sightseeing and adventure activities.

🌧️ Monsoon (Jul – Sep)

Beautiful greenery but travel carefully due to rains.

❄️ Winter (Oct – Feb)

Snowfall, skiing, and magical winter scenery.

Must Visit Places (Click to View)

🏔️ Solang Valley

Famous for adventure sports like paragliding, skiing, and snow rides. Offers panoramic views throughout the year.

❄️ Rohtang Pass

A high-altitude mountain pass known for snow-covered landscapes and breathtaking Himalayan views (open seasonally).

🛕 Hadimba Temple

A centuries-old wooden temple surrounded by cedar forests, dedicated to Goddess Hadimba.

🌄 Old Manali

Peaceful area with charming cafes, river walks, and a laid-back backpacker vibe.

♨️ Vashisht Hot Springs

Natural hot water springs believed to have healing properties, located near Vashisht village.

🚇 Atal Tunnel

The world’s longest highway tunnel above 10,000 ft, connecting Manali to the Lahaul-Spiti region.

3-Day Sample Itinerary

Day 1: Arrival, Hadimba Temple, Mall Road
Day 2: Solang Valley, Rohtang Pass / Atal Tunnel
Day 3: Old Manali, Vashisht Hot Springs, departure
